Keep chats with Protect

Protect saves your choice locally across reloads and prevents ChatSweeper from deleting the chat. Find protected chats with Show → Protected.

Protecting a selected chat deselects it. Protection applies only to ChatSweeper; it does not prevent manual deletion on Gemini or ChatGPT.
